FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Social Studying AI personalize learning?

It uses each learner's answers, confidence, and progress to adjust question difficulty, recommend the next topic, and reinforce concepts that need more practice.

Can students upload their own study materials?

Yes. Students can turn notes, PDFs, and slides into grounded questions, flashcards, and summaries in seconds.

Is the platform safe for school use?

Yes. School workspaces include approved-content grounding, moderation guardrails, roster controls, and educator visibility into student activity.

What can parents see?

Parents can follow mastery, streaks, focus time, and weekly progress without needing to monitor every individual study session.

Can schools try it before rolling it out?

Absolutely. Book a demo to explore the student and educator experience and discuss a rollout that fits your school.

How much does Social Studying AI cost?

Plans are tailored for individual learners, families, and schools. Book a demo to discuss the right option for your needs and rollout size.

How is student data protected?

Student information is handled with privacy-first controls, secure access, and clear workspace permissions so only authorized users can view learning data.
