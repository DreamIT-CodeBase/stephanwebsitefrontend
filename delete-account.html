Account & Data Deletion Request

Request the permanent deletion of your Social Studying AI account, study history, flashcards, questions, and all associated personal data in accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and App Store guidelines.

🛡️ What Happens Next

  • Your account is permanently scheduled for removal.
  • Study sets, flashcards, and history will be wiped.
  • A confirmation receipt is emailed to your address.
  • Complete database purge takes place within 30 days.

📱 In-App Deletion

You can also delete your account instantly from within the Android/iOS App: Navigate to Settings → Account Profile → Delete Account.
